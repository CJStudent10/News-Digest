## 2. Flights still catching up after Friday's nationwide ground stop
`Charlotte` · Covered by 3 outlets

A more than four-hour nationwide ground stop on Friday, the longest in 25 years, is still rippling through air travel. Officials blame an equipment outage linked to upgrading old technology, and the FAA says it doesn't believe it was a cyberattack. Charlotte Douglas saw more than 700 combined delays and cancellations on Saturday, with long rebooking lines.

**Key facts**
- The FAA expected about 5,000 cancellations nationwide Saturday
- About 1.5 million flyers had travel plans disrupted
- Charlotte had 351 cancellations and 405 delays Saturday, per FlightAware
- Most operations are expected to be back on track by Sunday night

**Worth knowing:** If you're flying soon, check your flight status before heading out. Things should be close to normal by Sunday night.

`Jamaica` · Covered by 2 outlets

**Key facts**

**Worth knowing:** Your email should be working again. Just don't click on any "restore your messages" emails.
