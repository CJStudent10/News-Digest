## 1. A Day of Firsts, Plus a Ruby-Red Gala in Kingston
`Jamaica` `Cute Animals` · Covered by 2 outlets

Good News Network looked back at November 21 in history. In 1974, Congress overrode President Gerald Ford's veto to pass the Freedom of Information Act, and in 1783 the world's first untethered hot-air balloon flight lifted off near Paris. Separately, the Jamaica Observer featured photos from the United Way of Jamaica's glamorous 40th-anniversary gala.

**Key facts**
- Congress overrode Ford's veto of FOIA with a two-thirds vote in 1974.
- The first untethered hot-air balloon flight lasted about 25 minutes and covered roughly 5.5 miles.
- Ella Fitzgerald debuted at the Apollo Theater at age 16 on this date in 1934.
- The United Way of Jamaica gala was held November 14 at the Jamaica Pegasus, themed "The Ruby Thread."

**Worth knowing:** FOIA still lets ordinary citizens request government records today, and the balloonists reportedly started the tradition of celebrating a flight with champagne.
