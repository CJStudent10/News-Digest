## 6. Free flu shots available across Jamaica
`Jamaica` `Health & Medicine` · Covered by 1 outlet

Jamaica is in the middle of flu season, and health officials are encouraging people to get vaccinated. The shot is free at public health centres and hospitals for priority groups, and anyone else can get it privately for a fee. Officials also recommend covering coughs and sneezes.

**Key facts**
- Flu season typically runs from October to March and can last into May
- Priority groups include pregnant women, healthcare workers, people 65 and older with chronic conditions, and children six months and older with chronic illnesses
- Annual vaccination is recommended

**Worth knowing:** It's an easy, free step that can help keep you and the people around you healthier this season.

- Charlotte City Council votes Monday on a working group to oversee those benefits

**Worth knowing:** Residents have a real early chance to weigh in, and the benefits could help fund things like a grocery store off West Boulevard.
