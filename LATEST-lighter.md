## 8. Jamaica's inflation eases slightly, but bills are still biting
`Jamaica` `US Headlines` · Covered by 1 outlet

Jamaica's annual inflation dipped to 8.2% in October from 8.6% in September, according to STATIN. Monthly prices rose 0.3%, driven mostly by higher electricity, water and sewage charges. Transport and food costs remain well above last year's levels.

**Key facts**
- Annual inflation: 8.2% in October, versus 2.9% a year earlier
- Transport costs are up 15.0% year over year
- Food prices are up 10.9%
- Electricity, gas and other fuels rose 3.5% in October alone

**Worth knowing:** The pace is easing a little, but household budgets are still feeling pressure from utilities, food and getting around.
