## 1. Trump says his deportation policy may have been 'too tough'
`US Headlines` `Jamaica` · Covered by 4 outlets

In a 60 Minutes interview after Republicans' heavy midterm losses, President Trump said his immigration crackdown 'both helped and hurt' his party and that deportations were 'too tough' on people with jobs whose only violation was entering illegally. He said he'd 'love to do something on immigration' with Democrats, without giving details. His own administration is sending mixed signals, though: the Department of Homeland Security says ICE is 'NOT backing away or slowing down,' and Trump just named immigration hardliner Stephen Miller as chief of staff.

**Key facts**
- Interview aired Sunday on CBS' 60 Minutes, with host Ross Douthat
- Trump gave himself an 'A plus' on reducing border crossings
- Election-day polling ranked immigration third among voter concerns, after the economy and the state of democracy
- Vice President JD Vance has floated focusing enforcement more on businesses that hire illegal labor

**Worth knowing:** It's one of Trump's clearest shifts in tone on a signature issue, and it could hint at whether an immigration deal with the new Democratic Congress is possible.
