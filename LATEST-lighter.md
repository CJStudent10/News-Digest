`Charlotte` `US Headlines` · Covered by 3 outlets

Democrat Chuck Hubbard has won North Carolina's 5th Congressional District, and longtime Republican Rep. Virginia Foxx conceded Sunday after a recount didn't change the outcome. Both candidates offered gracious words about the handoff.

**Key facts**
- Hubbard won by 330 votes out of about 287,000 cast
- Foxx had held the seat since first being elected in 2004 and chairs the House Rules Committee

- Democrats won 11 of North Carolina's 14 House seats, and Roy Cooper won a U.S. Senate seat

## 5. Democrats Reach 258 House Seats, the Biggest Majority This Century
`US Headlines` `Charlotte` · Covered by 2 outlets

With the North Carolina recount settled, House Democrats reached 258 seats, a 44-seat gain and the largest post-election majority for any party since 1992. Democrats will also control the Senate, so Washington will have divided government for the last two years of President Trump's term.

**Key facts**
- Republicans hold at least 173 seats, with four races still uncalled
- Hakeem Jeffries is set to become the first Black House speaker when Congress convenes in January
- Democratic leaders say they're planning a 100-day agenda focused on cost of living, plus investigations of the administration
- The Senate is also under Democratic control

**Worth knowing:** The balance of power in Congress is shifting in January, which will shape which bills move forward on things like prices and the economy.

## 7. Golding Asks PM Holness to Take Another Look at the Spring Mount Road
