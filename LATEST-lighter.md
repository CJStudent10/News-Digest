## 1. Thanksgiving storms soak both coasts, but clearer skies are coming
`US Headlines` · Covered by 3 outlets

Powerful storms hit the East Coast, the Midwest and the West Coast over Thanksgiving, causing flooding, fallen trees and widespread power outages. Hundreds of flights were cancelled or delayed during one of the busiest travel weeks of the year. Some bridges in New Jersey closed, and people were seen kayaking down streets in coastal Virginia. The storms are expected to move out and the tides to recede heading into Saturday.

**Key facts**
- About 490,000 customers were without power Friday afternoon, per poweroutage.us
- Nearly 201,000 of those were in New Jersey and Pennsylvania
- Very high 'King Tides' have been hitting the West Coast
- President Trump said he would consider releasing emergency funds

**Worth knowing:** If you're traveling home this weekend, check your flight and road conditions, though the weather should be improving by Saturday.

## 4. Jamaica's Justice Ministry rejects interference claim over legal honor
`Jamaica` · Covered by 1 outlet

## 6. Reggae Girlz book a third straight Women's World Cup trip
`Jamaica` · Covered by 1 outlet

Jamaica's women's team beat Costa Rica 3-0 in Texas to qualify for the 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup, their third in a row. The win also puts them in the W Championships semi-finals. Since Jamaica co-hosts the 2031 tournament, the team is now set for four straight World Cups.

**Key facts**
- Goals came from Jody Brown, Kalyssa Van Zanten and captain Khadija 'Bunny' Shaw
- Shaw's goal was her 68th in 50 appearances, and her seventh straight game with a goal
- The 2027 World Cup runs June 24 to July 25; the draw is Dec. 11 in Brazil

**Worth knowing:** A big, joyful milestone for Jamaican football, with a star captain on a hot streak.

## 8. Ras Michael revives a 1975 classic to help Hurricane Melissa recovery
