## 1. Thanksgiving storms leave hundreds of thousands without power
`US Headlines` `Jamaica` · Covered by 4 outlets

A powerful storm brought high winds, heavy rain and coastal flooding to the eastern US on Thanksgiving, while the West Coast dealt with its own storms and very high 'King Tides.' Power outages peaked near a million homes on Thursday evening, and roughly 490,000 customers were still without power Friday afternoon. Flights were badly disrupted too, but forecasters expect a much drier weekend in the Northeast as the storm moves into Canada.

**Key facts**
- About 490,000 customers were without power Friday afternoon, per PowerOutage.us; Pennsylvania was hardest hit.
- More than 2,500 US flights were delayed and over 450 cancelled on Thursday, according to FlightAware.
- It was the wettest Thanksgiving on record in Washington DC and at New York's Central Park, per preliminary data.
- Parts of Missouri got up to 15 inches of snow.

**Worth knowing:** If you're traveling home this weekend, conditions should improve, but another low-pressure system may arrive Monday.
