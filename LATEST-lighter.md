## 1. Netanyahu's rival gets first shot at forming Israel's next government
`US Headlines` · Covered by 3 outlets

Israeli President Isaac Herzog has asked Gadi Eisenkot, leader of the centrist Yashar party, to try to build a governing coalition after the Oct. 27 election. Netanyahu's Likud won the most seats, but his coalition fell short of a majority. Reporters call it the closest Netanyahu has come to losing power since 2021, though deep divisions could still stop a new government from forming.

**Key facts**
- Likud won 27 seats and Yashar won 21. A majority takes 61 of the Knesset's 120 seats.
- Netanyahu's coalition won 53 seats.
- Eisenkot has 28 days to form a coalition, with a possible 14-day extension.
- Netanyahu stays on as caretaker prime minister in the meantime.

**Worth knowing:** If Eisenkot can't form a government, another lawmaker gets a turn, and Israel could end up heading to another election.
