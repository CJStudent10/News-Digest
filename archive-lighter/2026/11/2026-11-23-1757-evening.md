## 1. Thanksgiving dinner costs a bit more, and one church is feeding 1,200
`US Headlines` `Charlotte` · Covered by 3 outlets

The American Farm Bureau says a classic Thanksgiving dinner for 10 costs about 6% more than last year, with turkey leading the rise. Wells Fargo's food institute thinks careful shoppers using sales could still come out slightly ahead. In Rock Hill, S.C., Illumine Church volunteers are shredding turkeys to deliver about 1,200 free meals on Thanksgiving Day.

**Key facts**
- Farm Bureau estimate: about $58.63 for 10 people (about $5.86 each), below 2022's record of $64.05
- A 16-pound frozen turkey averages about $23.68, up around 10%
- LendingTree survey: 51% plan to host this year, down from 59% last year
- Rock Hill families who need a meal can call (803) 810-9398

**Worth knowing:** Sticking to a list and shopping the deals can help, and about 100 Rock Hill volunteers are making sure their neighbors get a holiday plate.
