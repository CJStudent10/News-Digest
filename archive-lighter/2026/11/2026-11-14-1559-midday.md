## 1. Concord family safe after overnight house fire
`Charlotte` · Covered by 3 outlets

A two-story home on Millstone Circle SW in Concord caught fire just after midnight Saturday. Crews arrived within five minutes and had the fire under control in under an hour. No one was hurt, and the Red Cross is helping the family, who have had to leave their home.

**Key facts**
- Firefighters were dispatched at 12:21 a.m. and had the fire under control by 1:16 a.m.
- 36 firefighters from Concord and Harrisburg responded after a second alarm was called
- No injuries were reported
- The Concord Fire Marshal's Office is investigating the cause

**Worth knowing:** Fast work by local crews meant everyone got out safely, and the family is getting support while they recover.

## 3. Tired travelers at Charlotte airport after the ground stop
`Charlotte` · Covered by 2 outlets

After Friday's nearly five-hour nationwide ground stop, many travelers at Charlotte-Douglas spent the night on benches and floors, because rental cars and hotel rooms sold out fast. Passengers told WBTV they wished they had gotten more updates from airlines and the FAA. Several college football teams also had to adjust their travel and game plans.

**Key facts**
- The FAA lifted the ground stop after 8 p.m. Friday
- It was only the second nationwide ground stop of its kind since 9/11
- Federal officials said the effects would take at least through the weekend to clear
- Teams including Ole Miss, Kansas State and Utah had travel affected

**Worth knowing:** If you're flying this weekend, check your flight status before heading out and leave a little extra patience in your bag.

## 8. Couple turns grimy road signs sparkly as a hobby
`Cute Animals` · Covered by 1 outlet

Paul and Ruth Richmond-Watson of Brighton, England, noticed that many local road signs were too dirty to read at night, so they started cleaning them. They now scrub at least one sign a day and post the results on Instagram at @Filthy_signs. Both are autistic with ADHD, and they say the hobby helps their mental health and lets them give back to the community.

**Key facts**
- They started in September
- Each sign takes about 20 to 25 minutes, using eco-friendly foam and a long-handled brush
- They fund the work themselves, with a couple of sponsorships from supply companies
- They dream of a road trip to clean signs across the UK

**Worth knowing:** It's a small, satisfying act of neighborliness that makes roads a little safer, and they're clearly having a great time doing it.
