## 1. Meet Jamaica's 2027 Rhodes Scholar
`Jamaica` · Covered by 2 outlets

Somesh Sharma, 22, from Ocho Rios, St Ann, was named Jamaica's 2027 Rhodes Scholar at King's House on Thursday. He'll head to Oxford to study medical physics and digital health, hoping to bring better medical technology back home. His interest grew from watching his grandmother struggle to get proper care after a heart attack.

**Key facts**
- Graduated from UWI Mona in medical physics with first-class honours
- Will pursue an MSc in Medical Physics with Radiobiology and Applied Digital Health at Oxford
- Won on his first try, from a competitive group of candidates
- Built free apps including Yaad Bites and an Outbreak Tracker with about 5,000 users in 40 countries

**Worth knowing:** A young person with a clear plan to give back is a lovely thing to root for, and he graciously said he wished there were more awards for his fellow finalists.
