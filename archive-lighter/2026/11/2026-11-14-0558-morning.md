## 1. Old equipment grounds every US flight for four hours
`Jamaica` · Covered by 4 outlets

The FAA halted departures at all US airports Friday afternoon after a telecommunications outage hit regional air traffic control centers. The airspace reopened that evening, but the backlog spilled into Saturday with thousands of cancellations. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy blamed aging 'legacy' equipment and urged Congress to fund upgrades. Officials said the public was never in danger.

**Key facts**
- The ground stop began around 4:11 p.m. Friday, Nov. 13, and was lifted by 8:15 p.m.
- FlightAware reported more than 10,000 delays Friday and nearly 3,300 cancellations by early Saturday.
- Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said there was no evidence of a cyberattack and no link to Thursday's Microsoft outage, though investigators are still checking.
- A day earlier, Duffy asked Congress for $30 billion to modernize air traffic control.

**Worth knowing:** If you're flying soon, especially with Thanksgiving coming, check with your airline before heading to the airport. Officials expect delays while things catch up.
