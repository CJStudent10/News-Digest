## 1. Judge hits pause on Trump's planned D.C. arch
`US Headlines` · Covered by 4 outlets

A federal planning commission approved President Trump's 250-foot 'triumphal arch' near Arlington National Cemetery on Thursday by an 8-4 vote. Less than two hours later, Judge Tanya Chutkan ordered a 14-day pause on construction while lawsuits from D.C., Virginia and a group of veterans move forward. Supporters call it a tribute to US history and veterans for the country's 250th anniversary, while opponents say it would block views of the cemetery and needs approval from Congress.

**Key facts**
- The National Capital Planning Commission voted 8-4 after nearly nine hours of public comment.
- Judge Chutkan's temporary restraining order bars construction for 14 days.
- The arch would stand on Memorial Circle, between the Lincoln Memorial and Arlington National Cemetery.
- Officials said work would run 20 hours a day for two to three years.

**Worth knowing:** It's a live test of how big new monuments get approved in the capital, and the courts will now weigh whether Congress needs to sign off.
