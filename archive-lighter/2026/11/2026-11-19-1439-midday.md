## 1. Diesel spill reaches Little Sugar Creek near Uptown Charlotte
`Charlotte` · Covered by 3 outlets

A car crash on Wednesday spilled about 80 gallons of diesel, and some of it made its way into Little Sugar Creek near Uptown. Cleanup crews are on it, but officials say petroleum spills take time to fully clean up.

**Key facts**
- About 80 gallons of diesel spilled after a crash on Nov. 18
- The crash happened at North McDowell and East 6th streets
- Cleanup will continue until compliance standards are met
- Residents can report suspected pollution by calling 311

**Worth knowing:** If you're near the creek and notice anything odd you can see or smell, a quick 311 call helps the cleanup.
